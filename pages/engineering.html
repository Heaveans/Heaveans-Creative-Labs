Engineering

We Build to Find Out.

Engineering turns questions into systems.

At Heaveans Creative Labs, we explore the intersection of electronics, software, artificial intelligence, signal processing, sensing and emerging technologies.

Some work begins as research.

Some becomes a prototype.

Some becomes a platform.

The objective is not to pretend every experiment is production-ready. The objective is to discover what works.
