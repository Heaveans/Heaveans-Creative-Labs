About Heaveans

A Creative Lab for Ideas That Don't Fit Into One Box.

Heaveans Creative Labs is a multidisciplinary creative-technology and innovation studio from Kenya.

We explore the space between creativity and technology — combining design thinking with engineering, artificial intelligence, experimentation and research.

Our work spans visual communication, digital products, intelligent systems, connected technologies, climate innovation, telecommunications and aerospace.

We are interested in ideas that cross boundaries.

Because some problems cannot be solved from a single discipline.

Why Heaveans?

Design × Engineering × Intelligence

We do not see these disciplines as separate worlds.

Design helps technology communicate.

Engineering makes ideas tangible.

Artificial intelligence creates new possibilities.

Together, they create systems that can be understood, tested and improved.

Our Philosophy

Explore Before You Assume.

Explore Before You Assume.

Good ideas deserve to be tested. We prefer evidence over assumption and experimentation over speculation.

Build Before You Overpromise.

A prototype can reveal more than a presentation. Honest experimentation is more valuable than making claims a system cannot yet support.

Connect Disciplines.

Design can improve engineering. Engineering can unlock creativity. Intelligence can connect both. The most interesting opportunities often exist between disciplines.

Start Where You Are.

African innovation does not need to imitate another environment. It can begin with the realities, constraints and opportunities around us.

Keep Asking.

The Lab exists because there are always better questions to investigate.
