Current Research Areas

Wavelet-Based Signal Detection

Investigating wavelet-based techniques for identifying patterns and anomalies within complex signals.

GNSS Signal Monitoring

Exploring satellite-navigation signals, digital signal processing and methods for understanding signal behaviour and disturbances.

Radar Rainfall Classification

Exploring radar, artificial intelligence and signal processing for rainfall classification and weather intelligence.

Spectrum-Aware Connectivity

Investigating geospatial and telecommunications systems for more intelligent use of wireless spectrum.

How We Work

Engineering Philosophy

Prototype Before You Overpromise.

A working prototype can reveal limitations that a presentation cannot.

Design for Reality.

Technology must account for the environments in which it will actually operate.

Measure What Matters.

Engineering decisions should be grounded in evidence, testing and measurable performance.

Stay Curious.

Every experiment should produce either a useful result or a better question.
